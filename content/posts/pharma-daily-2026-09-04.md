## One Take

Three of the window's four largest checks — GSK-Hutchmed ($110M/$1.3B), Menarini-Gan & Lee ($771M), NeuShen ($80M) — went to China-originated assets, and the ivonescimab OS readout says the data behind them is holding up in a head-to-head against the world's best-selling drug. Hutchmed's +17.8% two-day move on ~9x volume says Hong Kong priced the deal as validation, not just cash. My read: the pricing discount on China-originated BD assets keeps compressing, and upfront share (8.5% here vs the classic 5-10% band) is where I'd watch it. Conviction: moderate — this is one window and a 2-deal comp base. What would change my mind: milestone payments stalling on 2024-25 vintage deals, or a high-profile China-asset Phase 3 failure in the next two quarters.

## Key takeaways

- GSK-Hutchmed: $110M upfront against up to $1.3B total — an 8.5% upfront share, inside the classic 5-10% band for licenses.
- Medicus Pharma's license carries just 1.5% upfront ($15M of $1B) — read headline totals as optionality, not revenue.
- Ivonescimab cleared the OS bar in a Phase 3 head-to-head vs Keytruda — the strongest single data point behind the China-asset re-rating.
- Hutchmed rose +17.8% over Sep 2→4 on ~9x average volume; US-listed deal tickers barely moved (CLYM +6.4%, INBX −0.9%).
